Privacy

Last updated 27 September 2026

Expense Tracker is open-source software that people run on their own servers. This page covers this website, its live demo, and what the software does with Google data wherever it runs.

This website and the demo

  • The demo never asks for a Google account. Everyone who tries it gets a private sandbox of made-up bank alerts, which is deleted after an hour.
  • The only cookies are the demo's sign-in cookies, which keep your sandbox open while you use it. There is no analytics, advertising or tracking.
  • The server keeps ordinary request logs for up to 30 days to diagnose problems.

What the software does with Google data

When someone signs in to an installation of Expense Tracker with Google, it asks for three things:

  • Your name and email address, to identify your account.
  • Read-only access to Gmail, used only to find bank transaction alerts and statements. It cannot send, change or delete email.
  • Access to files it creates in your Google Drive, used to keep your ledger in one file there. It cannot see any other file in your Drive.

The text of bank alert and statement emails is sent to Claude (Anthropic's AI model, through Anthropic's API or Amazon Bedrock, whichever the installation is set up to use) to read the amount, merchant and category. Each installation's sign-in screens name the one it uses. Once the app has learned a bank's alert format, it reads that format itself without the model.

The installation's server keeps only your Google account id, email, name, sign-in timestamps and an encrypted Google sign-in token. Your transactions, rules, loans, IOUs and PIN live in your own Drive file.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Expense Tracker's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Gmail and Drive data is used only to provide the features you see in the app. It is not used for advertising, not sold, not used to assess creditworthiness, and not read by people.

Your control

  • Delete your account from the app's settings. You can choose to delete the ledger file in your Drive too.
  • Revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Export every spend as CSV from the app's settings.
